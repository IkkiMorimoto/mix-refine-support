Audio and settings

Mix Refine prepares, plays and analyzes imported audio on your device. Audio files, waveform caches, loudness results, cue positions and settings are stored within the app. We do not operate an audio upload server and do not receive these files or measurements.

File providers

When you select audio from iCloud Drive, Dropbox or another provider through Files, that provider handles access to the source under its own privacy policy. Mix Refine copies the selected file into the app for playback. Recent-file and favorite shortcuts save filenames, source locations and access bookmarks locally on your device. Removing a shortcut does not delete the source file.

Purchases

Apple handles optional support purchases and offer-code redemption. Mix Refine checks Apple-verified transactions to enable UI customization. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple manages purchase history and restoration under its policies.

Tracking and analytics

Mix Refine has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may process crash diagnostics and TestFlight feedback through its services under its own policies.

Feedback

If you choose to contact support or send TestFlight feedback, we may receive what you send and use it to investigate your report. Avoid sharing confidential audio unless you intend to provide it.

Retention and deletion

Imported audio, cached audio and settings are kept in local app storage. Some cached files may remain after replacing a track. Deleting the app removes its local audio and settings; device backups and file-provider data are managed separately by iOS and those providers. Deleting the app does not erase your Apple purchase history. Contact support about information you have sent directly to us.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not add advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. You can contact us using the email link above.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes. Use the support page linked from the app's App Store listing to contact us.
